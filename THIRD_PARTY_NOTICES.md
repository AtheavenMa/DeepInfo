# Research references and asset provenance

DeepInfo is an independent clean implementation of the methods from the peer-reviewed research paper by Ma et al. (2022). See [paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ma22b.html) and the [historical reference code](https://github.com/haotianSustc/deepinfo). Its noise-optimization engineering is also informed by the [SegJointGene CID application](https://github.com/daifengwanglab/segjointgene). Those upstream repositories' source files are not copied or vendored in this repository.

The paper and official conference slides have separate publication rights. The original [ICML 2022 presentation](https://icml.cc/media/icml-2022/Slides/17920.pdf) is linked, not redistributed as figures in this repository. The script `scripts/extract_paper_figures.py` records the source and selection of each slide panel.

`assets/examples/colored_disk.npz`, `assets/checkpoints/tiny_cnn.pt`, and the demo visualizations were generated by this repository's scripts using synthetic inputs. No third-party input image, weights, or pretrained model are redistributed.

Runtime dependencies are installed from PyPI and retain their respective licenses; dependency sources are not vendored here.
